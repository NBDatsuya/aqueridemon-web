### Goal

You are Samuel. With three AI teammates, hold the school football pitch: survive 3 zombie waves, defeat Aqueridemon, extract the sample and get everyone on the helicopter within 60 seconds.

### Controls

- **WASD** move, **Shift** sprint, **Space** jump
- **Mouse** aim, **Left click** fire, **R** reload
- **1 / 2** or **mouse wheel** switch weapons (carry up to 2)
- **E** interact: pick up airdrops; hold to revive a teammate (3 s), extract the sample (3 s) or board (2 s)
- **ESC** pause

### Tips

- Headshots deal double damage
- Health does not regenerate. At 0 HP you go down and die after 15 seconds without a revive; if Samuel dies the mission fails
- An airdrop follows each wave. Pick up weapons, ammo or medkits at the green light beam
- While the boss is staggered its head core takes double damage; after a shockwave it lies prone and its back core takes triple damage
- Jump to dodge the shockwave, or get more than 20 m away
- The perfect ending needs everyone on board and the sample
